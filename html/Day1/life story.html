My Life Journey – From a Small Village to Big Dreams My life journey began in a small village. There are five members in my family: my father, my mother, my younger brother, my younger sister, and me. My family is the greatest strength in my life. Their love and support always give me the courage to move forward. Before coming to Chennai, I lived in a village. During my childhood, I rarely went outside my home. In my village, girls usually studied only up to 10th grade. But I wanted to continue my education and build a better future for myself. Coming to Chennai became a major turning point in my life. I completed my 11th and 12th grades in Chennai. Everything was new to me—the city, the people, and the environment. Even though it was challenging, I continued my studies with confidence. After completing my school education, I went to Coimbatore to continue my higher studies. I pursued a Diploma in Computer Science Engineering and successfully completed it in 2023. This was an important achievement in my life because it gave me more confidence to face new challenges. After completing my diploma, I returned to Chennai and started studying B.A. Political Science. I am completing my degree in 2026. Studying Political Science after Computer Science was a new experience for me, but I believe it will help me prepare for my future goal of clearing the UPSC examination. I have two major dreams in my life. One is to build a successful career in the IT field. My biggest dream is to clear the UPSC examination and serve the people and the country. There was a time when I was hesitant to even step outside my home. Today, I have travelled to different cities, continued my education, and started building my own future. When I look back at my journey, I feel proud of how far I have come. My journey has not always been easy, and there may be many challenges ahead. But I will never give up on my dreams. I will continue to work hard for myself, my family, and my future. One day, I want to achieve my dreams and proudly say: “No matter how difficult my journey was, I never gave up.” My story is not over yet. This is not the end of my journey; it is the beginning of my biggest dreams.
